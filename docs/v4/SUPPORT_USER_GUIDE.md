# Relay support — user guide (V4)

Contact Relay's own support team from inside Relay. This is separate from your social inbox: messages go to Relay staff,
never to your audience or through your social accounts.

## Where
- Sidebar **Help** (phones: **More → Help**), or the account menu → **Contact support** / **My requests**.
- Help has two pages in its left menu: **Contact support** and **My requests**.

## Contact support
1. Choose a category: Account access, Billing, Publishing, Connections, Analytics, Technical problem, Feature request, or
   **Security or private account matter** (private: only you and people you add can see it — workspace owners can't).
2. Add a subject and message. Attach screenshots or files if useful: PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, PDF or plain text, up to 10 MB
   each, five per message. Files are checked before they can be sent.
3. **Include technical details** (on by default, untick to leave out): app version, browser family, the page you came
   from (path only) and recent error references. Nothing else is collected — no cookies, tokens, storage or drafts.
4. **Send**. You get a reference such as `RLY-482133`.

Credentials pasted into a message (passwords, API keys, tokens, card numbers) are redacted before anyone sees them.

## My requests
- Open / Resolved / All, search by text or reference. Owners and admins can switch to **Whole workspace**.
- A dot marks a new reply from Relay. Statuses: Received, With Relay, Awaiting your reply, Resolved, Closed.
- Open a request to read and reply, **Mark resolved**, or **Reopen** (within 30 days of resolution; replying to a resolved
  request also reopens it). Closed (archived) requests are read-only — start a new request.
- **People → Add** (requester or an admin, with access to the member list): share the request with a colleague.
- **Transcript** downloads the conversation as text.

## Notifications
When Relay replies you get an in-app notification (category **Support**) and, if email is set up and your preferences allow
("Relay support replies" in notification settings), an email with the reference and a link — never the message text.
Replying by email is not supported; reply in Relay.

## Support access
If Relay asks to look at your workspace, the request shows on the ticket. Owners/admins can **Approve** (read-only, for the
requested time, confirmed with a fresh sign-in), **Decline**, or later **End access**. See `SUPPORT_ACCESS_WORKFLOW.md`.

## Problem reports
**Report a problem** (account menu) saves a redacted report for your workspace admins only. To involve Relay, choose
**Send to Relay support** (after sending a report, or from Admin → Security and audit → Problem reports): review what will
be shared, untick anything you don't want to send, then send. It becomes a request (or is added to one of your open requests).

## While billing restricts your workspace
Support (and billing) stay available when a payment problem or an ended trial restricts paid actions.
