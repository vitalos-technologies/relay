There are two ways into Relay: start your own workspace (on Free, or with a free trial of a paid plan), or accept an
invitation to a workspace that already exists. If your organisation already uses Relay, ask a workspace admin to invite
you instead.

## Start free or start a free trial

**Before you start:** you need an email address you can sign in with. No card is needed.

1. On the Relay home page, choose **Start free trial** or **Start free** (or a plan in the pricing section). The sign-up
   page opens.
2. Pick a plan: **Free** (£0 for one person, no trial), **Standard** (£5 per user per month after the 14-day trial) or
   **Professional** (£15 per user per month after the trial, with advanced workflows). You can change the plan during a
   trial.
3. Choose **Continue with Free** or **Start free trial on <Plan>**.
4. Sign in, or create your account when asked.
5. On **Create your workspace**, enter a **Workspace name** (for example your company name) and pick the **Time zone**
   your team plans in.
6. Choose **Create workspace** (Free) or **Start free trial**.

**Result:** your workspace is created and you're its **owner**. On Free, Relay opens the Overview. On a trial, Relay opens
**Settings → Billing** so you can see the trial end date. A trial that ends without a subscription moves the workspace to
Free; nothing is deleted.

## Create a workspace from onboarding

If you sign in without a workspace (for example you came straight to the sign-in page), Relay shows **Create your
workspace** with these fields:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Workspace name | Your organisation's name. You can change it later in Settings. |
| First brand (optional) | The first client, product or business you post for. |
| Time zone | The workspace's default time zone. Each brand can have its own later. |

Choose **Create**. The workspace starts on **Free**; upgrade from **Billing** when you need more. If you named a first
brand, Relay opens **Connections** so you can connect that brand's social accounts. Otherwise it opens the brand settings
so you can create one.

## Join with an invitation

If a colleague invited you, open the link in the invitation email and sign in with the same email address the invitation
was sent to. See [Accept an invitation](/help/teams-and-approvals/accept-an-invitation).

## Common errors

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **You already have a workspace** | Your account already belongs to a workspace, so you can't start another trial. | Open **Billing** in that workspace to choose a plan, or ask its owner. |
| **No workspace yet** · ask an admin to invite you | Creating new workspaces isn't open for your account. | Ask an admin of your organisation's workspace to invite you. |
| **Enter a name.** | The workspace name is empty. | Type a name, then try again. |
| **Use the invited email address.** | You signed in with a different email from the one invited. | Sign out and sign in with the invited address. |

## Limitations

- One free trial per workspace. The trial doesn't renew.
- People who already belong to a workspace can't create a new one themselves.
- Prices are in GBP. Any tax is shown at checkout when you subscribe.
