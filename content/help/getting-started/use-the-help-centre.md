The Help Centre is at **Help** in the app menu (Workspace group) and at `/help`. Anyone can read the general articles;
sign in to see the articles for your role.

## Find an article

1. Type in **Search help**. Suggestions appear as you type.
2. Use **↑** and **↓** to move through suggestions and **Enter** to open one. Press **Enter** without choosing a
   suggestion to see all results.
3. Or browse a section from the Help home page or the list of sections on the left.

Press **/** anywhere in the Help Centre to jump to the search box.

Each article shows the Relay version it describes and when it was last reviewed. Articles about features that need extra
setup, or approval from a social network, say so at the top.

## What you can see

| You are | You see |
|---|---|
| Not signed in | General articles, such as this one |
| A workspace member | Articles for members, plus developer documentation |
| A workspace owner or admin | Everything, including administration articles |

Search only finds articles you're allowed to read.

## Help next to settings

Some settings, metrics and error messages have a **Help** link beside them. It opens the matching article in a new tab,
so you don't lose what you were doing.

## Offline copy

Signed-in members can choose **Download offline copy** on the Help home page. You get a zip of HTML pages with the
articles you're allowed to read. The Help Centre in the app is always the most recent version.

## Contact Relay support

If you can't find an answer, choose **Contact support** on the Help home page (or in the account menu). Signed-in members
can follow and reply to their requests in **My requests**.

## Limitations

- Articles describe Relay as it works today. Features that aren't available aren't described as if they were.
- Rules that belong to social networks (for example video lengths) can change. Articles link to the network's own page
  with the date we last checked it.
