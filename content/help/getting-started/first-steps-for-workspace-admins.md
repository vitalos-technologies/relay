Use this checklist after you [create your workspace](/help/getting-started/create-your-workspace). Each step can be done
later, but publishing works best when brands, channels, roles and approvals are in place before the team starts.

**Who can do this:** the workspace **owner** and **admins**. Some steps need an owner (noted below). Relay may ask you to
verify your sign-in again (two-step verification) before sensitive changes.

## 1. Create your brands

A brand groups the social accounts, posts, time zone and rules for one client, product or business line.

1. Open **Settings → Workspace → Brands** ([/settings/workspace/branding](/settings/workspace/branding)).
2. Add a brand for each client or business line. Set its time zone if it differs from the workspace.

## 2. Connect social accounts

1. Open **Connections → Social accounts**.
2. Connect each brand's accounts. See [Connect social accounts](/help/social-connections/connect-social-accounts).

Some networks need Relay's network app to be set up and approved first. If a network shows **Not set up**, see
[Set up social network apps](/help/social-connections/set-up-social-network-apps).

## 3. Invite people and give them roles

1. Open **Team** and choose **Invite**.
2. Give each person a role for the whole workspace or only for some brands.

Start with the narrowest role that lets someone do their job. See [Invite people](/help/teams-and-approvals/invite-people)
and [Understand roles and permissions](/help/teams-and-approvals/roles-and-permissions).

## 4. Decide how posts get approved

1. Open **Settings → Publishing → Approval policies** ([/settings/publishing/approvals](/settings/publishing/approvals)).
2. Choose which brands need approval and how many approval stages they have.
3. Under **Settings → Publishing → Defaults**, decide whether editors may publish without review.

See [Approval policies and stages](/help/teams-and-approvals/approval-policies-and-stages).

## 5. Secure the workspace (owner)

1. Open **Settings → People and access → Workspace security**
   ([/settings/people/security-policy](/settings/people/security-policy)).
2. Require two-step verification for the roles that can publish, change settings or manage people.

See [Workspace security policy](/help/account-security/workspace-security-policy).

## 6. Check plan and billing

**Settings → Workspace → Plan and billing** shows your plan, the trial end date and the seats you're paying for.

## Expected outcome

Your team can sign in, sees only the brands they work on, and every post goes through the approvals you chose before
it's sent.

## Limitations

- Only the owner can change the workspace security policy and transfer ownership.
- Admins can't give anyone more access than they hold themselves.
