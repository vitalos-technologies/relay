This is the shortest path from a new workspace to a published post. Each step links to the full article.

**Before you start**

- Your workspace has at least one **brand**.
- You can create posts (for example as an editor) and, to publish or schedule, you're a publisher, brand manager, admin
  or owner. Editors can write and submit posts for review.
- An admin has connected a social account to the brand, or you can do it yourself if you manage connections.

## 1. Connect a social account

1. Open **Connections** (Workspace group in the menu).
2. On **Social accounts**, choose the network and follow its sign-in screens.
3. Pick the page, profile or channel Relay should post to.

**Result:** the account appears as a channel of the brand with the status **Ready**. Some networks need extra setup
or approval first. See [Connect social accounts](/help/social-connections/connect-social-accounts).

## 2. Write the post

1. Choose **Create**.
2. Pick one or more channels.
3. Write the text. Add images or a video if you want.
4. Check the preview for each channel. If a network needs different text or media, edit that channel's version.

See [Create a post](/help/publishing/create-a-post) and
[Shared copy and channel versions](/help/publishing/shared-copy-and-channel-versions).

## 3. Send it, schedule it or ask for review

- **Publish now** sends it straight away.
- **Schedule** sends it at a date and time you pick, in the brand's time zone unless you choose another.
- If the brand requires approval, **Submit for review** instead. It's scheduled or published only after approval. See
  [Submit a post for review](/help/teams-and-approvals/submit-a-post-for-review).

## 4. Check what happened

- The **Planner** shows the post at its time. Open it to see each channel's delivery.
- **Overview → Needs attention** lists anything that failed or couldn't be confirmed.

**Expected outcome:** each channel shows **Published** with a link to the live post. If one channel failed and others
succeeded, only the failed one needs attention — Relay never sends the successful ones again.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No channels to pick | Connect an account to the brand first, or ask an admin. |
| **Publish now** or **Schedule** isn't available | Your role can't publish this brand, or the brand needs approval. Submit it for review. |
| A channel shows a validation warning | Fix the text or media for that channel (for example it's too long, or a video is required). |
| A channel shows **Unknown — needs review** (**Not confirmed** on Overview) | The network didn't confirm whether the post went out. Check the live account before trying again, so it isn't posted twice. |
