Relay helps a team **create, coordinate and publish** social media posts from one place. You write a post once, adjust it
for each network, get it approved if your workspace needs that, and schedule it. Relay then sends it at the right time and
tells you what happened.

## What you can do

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your home page: what's coming up, what needs attention and how recent posts performed. |
| **Planner** | A calendar of scheduled and published posts across your channels. |
| **Create** | Write a post, add images or video, preview it for each network and publish or schedule it. |
| **Content library** | Drafts, media, templates and imported content in one place. |
| **Campaigns** | Group related posts and follow them together. |
| **Approvals** | Review posts before they go out, if your workspace requires it. |
| **Inbox** | Reply to comments and messages from connected accounts, and track cases. |
| **Listening** | Follow mentions and topics on connected sources. |
| **Assistant** | AI help for drafting, plus repeatable AI workflows and automation. |
| **Analytics** | Reach, engagement and other metrics, by network and by post. |
| **Team, Connections, Settings** | People and roles, connected social accounts and business apps, and workspace settings. |

What you see depends on your role. If an area is missing from your menu, you don't have access to it in this workspace.

## How Relay is organised

- A **workspace** is your organisation's account in Relay. People, billing and settings belong to a workspace.
- A workspace has one or more **brands**. A brand groups the social accounts, posts and settings for one client, product
  or business line.
- Each connected social account is a **channel**. A post can go to several channels at once.
- People get **roles** (for example editor, approver or publisher) for the whole workspace or for specific brands.

See the [glossary](/help/getting-started/glossary) for other terms.

## What Relay doesn't do for you

- Relay publishes only to accounts you connect, and only within each network's own rules. Some networks need extra
  approval from the network before Relay can publish or read analytics for them.
- Analytics come from the networks. Numbers can arrive late, and some networks don't report every metric.
- Relay never posts on its own: every post is created, approved or scheduled by someone in your team, or by an automation
  someone in your team set up.
