Relay's menu is grouped by what you're doing. You only see the items your role allows in the current workspace; the
server checks every action again, so a hidden item is never just "turned off" in your browser.

## The main menu

| Group | Items | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Publish** | Overview, Planner, Create, Content library, Campaigns, Approvals | **Create** needs permission to edit drafts. **Approvals** shows a count of posts waiting for you. |
| **Engage** | Inbox, Listening, Assistant | Each appears only if you have access to it on at least one brand. |
| **Insights** | Analytics | Needs permission to read analytics. |
| **Workspace** | Tasks, Team, Connections, Settings, Help | **Connections** has two tabs: Social accounts and Business apps. |
| **Admin** | Admin console, Setup, Operations, Audit log | Only for people with admin rights. **Setup** is for the workspace owner. |

On a large screen the menu is a full sidebar. On a tablet-sized screen it shrinks to a row of icons; hover or focus an
icon to see its name.

## The top bar

From left to right:

- **Brand filter** — show one brand or **All brands**. Most pages follow this filter.
- **Search** — find posts, media, campaigns and settings, or jump to a page. Press **Ctrl+K** (Windows, Linux) or
  **⌘K** (Mac) from anywhere.
- **Notifications** — mentions, replies, review requests and task updates.
- **Account menu** (your initials) — appearance (System, Dark or Light), **Profile and settings**, **Security**,
  **What's new**, **Help Centre**, **Contact support**, **My requests**, **Report a problem** and **Sign out**.

The **workspace switcher** sits at the top of the sidebar. See
[Switch workspaces and brands](/help/teams-and-approvals/switch-workspaces-and-brands).

If you're allowed to stop publishing in an emergency, **Pause publishing** sits at the bottom of the sidebar. It holds
every scheduled post in the workspace until someone resumes publishing.

## On a phone

- The menu moves to a bar at the bottom of the screen. It shows the two to four pages you're most likely to use, based on
  your role — for example **Overview · Planner · Create · Approvals** for people who publish, or **Overview · Inbox** for
  customer care.
- Everything else is under **More**, in the same groups as the desktop menu.

## Search tips

- In **Search** (Ctrl+K / ⌘K), use **↑** and **↓** to move, **Enter** to open and **Esc** to close.
- With an empty search box you see quick actions and the items you opened recently.
- Results only include things you're allowed to open.

## Why can't I see something?

- **A menu item is missing:** your role in this workspace doesn't include it. See
  [Understand roles and permissions](/help/teams-and-approvals/roles-and-permissions).
- **A brand is missing from the brand filter:** you don't have a role on that brand.
- **You see a different workspace's content than expected:** check the workspace switcher. Each browser tab follows the
  workspace you last chose; other open tabs reload when you switch.
