## Saved replies

**Create (admins, `inbox.manage`):** **Settings → Inbox → Saved replies** ([/settings/inbox/saved-replies](/settings/inbox/saved-replies))
→ new saved reply → **Title** and **Text** → **Save**. **Edit** later the same way.

**Use (anyone who can reply):** insert a saved reply from the reply box, adjust it for the customer, then send.

Saved replies are a starting point; Relay doesn't send them automatically.

## Saved views

1. In the inbox, set filters (queue, type, channel, priority, search).
2. Under **Saved views**, select **Save current filters**, give it a **Name** (*e.g. Urgent reviews*), **Save**.
3. Pick it later from **Views**. Delete it with **Delete view …**.
