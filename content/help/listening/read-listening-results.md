Open **Listening** ([/listening](/listening)). The header says what's monitored (*Monitoring 3 connected channels · Public
comments, mentions and reviews since …*); **Simulated** marks demo data. *Collection incomplete* lists channels that
weren't collecting and why.

## A saved search

Open a search to see **Mentions** (with the previous period, or *No full previous period*), **Negative**, **Uncertain**,
**Repeats collapsed**, **Sentiment**, **Topics** and the list of **Mentions** (searchable, filter by sentiment or topic).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mention** | A public comment, mention or review from a monitored source that matches the search. |
| **Unique mentions** | Repeats collapsed: identical text (ignoring links, @handles, punctuation, case and accents) within 7 days counts once. A repeat shows *Repeat of an earlier mention (counted once)*. |
| **Sentiment** | **Inferred, not fact.** A transparent word-list method; low confidence is shown as *uncertain* with "?". Shares use scored unique mentions only. |
| **Topics** | Words and pairs that appear together in several mentions (search terms excluded). Groups overlap. *Not enough mentions to group yet* with few mentions. |
| **Share of voice** | *Each search's mentions ÷ the sum across compared searches, over the same sources and window. Observed data only.* |
| **Not collected** | Periods before monitoring began, drawn as a gap — never as zero. |

## Compare

**Compare** (on the overview or a search's **Compare…**) puts an own-brand search next to competitor searches. Share of
voice only uses sources that all compared searches cover.

## Act on a result

From a mention, alert or search: **Create task**, **Create content draft**, or (from a mention's menu) open its
conversation or a **case** (needs inbox and case access). Only a link to the mention is copied, never the customer's text.

## Corrections

Managers can exclude a mention from all metrics (reversible, audited). When a customer deletes or edits a comment on the
network, Relay removes or updates it in listening too.
