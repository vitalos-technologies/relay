**Required:** `ai.use` plus access to what you're working on; an AI provider set up for your Relay installation.

## Where it appears

| Page | What you can ask |
|---|---|
| **Create** (composer) | Draft or adapt copy for a destination, translate, write a brief, ask about the post. **Use** puts the text into the chosen version. |
| **Inbox** | Summarise the conversation, suggest a reply (goes into the reply box, not sent), suggest labels and priority, translate. |
| **Planner**, **Content library** | Brief, copy, questions. |
| **Analytics** | Explain the numbers shown, with the period and evidence; turn an insight into a draft or task. |
| **Listening** | Summarise mentions with evidence and coverage. |

## What it never does

- It never sends, publishes, exports, or changes external records on its own.
- It only reads what **you** are allowed to see, checked again when it runs.
- Discussions, tasks and notifications are never sent to it.
- Customer messages and documents are treated as untrusted data: instructions hidden in them are ignored and reported.

## Messages you may see

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AI is not set up | No AI provider configured. Ask an Owner. |
| AI budget used up / Budget used up | The workspace's AI budget for the month is spent (see [AI limits](/help/ai-and-mcp/ai-limits-privacy-and-usage)). |
| Paid actions are paused | Billing restriction; AI requests are refused. |
| A rate-limit message | 20 requests per 10 minutes per person; wait a moment. |
| Declined | The assistant declined the request (for example it couldn't answer from the allowed sources). |
| Stopped | Your access changed or a policy stopped the run. |
