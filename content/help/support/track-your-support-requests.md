1. Open **Help → My requests** ([/help/requests](/help/requests)).
2. Filter by **Status** (Open, Resolved, All) and search by text or reference (*Search or RLY-…*). Owners and admins can
   switch **Whose requests** to **Whole workspace** (requests marked **Private** stay hidden).
3. A **New reply** marker shows unread answers from Relay.

## Statuses

**Received** · **With Relay** · **Awaiting your reply** · **Resolved** · **Closed**.

## Inside a request

- **Reply** (*Write a reply…*). Replying to a resolved request reopens it (*Reply to reopen this request…*).
- **Mark resolved** or **Reopen** (within 30 days of resolution).
- **People → Add someone to this request**: share it with a colleague who can see the member list.
- **Transcript** downloads the conversation as text.
- A **closed** request is read-only: *This request is closed.* → **Start a new request**.
