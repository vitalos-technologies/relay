1. Open the account menu → **Report a problem**, describe what happened and send.
2. **Result:** a redacted report is saved for **your workspace's admins only** (Admin console → Security and audit →
   Problem reports).

## Send it to Relay

After sending, or from **Problem reports** (admins), choose **Send to Relay support**:

1. Review what will be shared (**Shared as**, technical details, **Draft excerpt**) and untick anything under **Include**
   you don't want to send. *No cookies, tokens, storage, logs or other drafts are sent.*
2. **Send as** a **New request** or add it to one of your open requests; add a **Message**.
3. Select **Send to Relay**.
