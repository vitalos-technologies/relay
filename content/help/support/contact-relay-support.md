Relay support is Relay's own team. It's separate from your **Inbox**: these messages never go to your audience or through
your social accounts. Every member can contact support, and support stays available even when billing restricts paid actions.

## Send a request

1. Open **Help → Contact support** ([/help/contact](/help/contact)), or the account menu → **Contact support**. On phones:
   **More → Help**.
2. Choose a **Category**: Account access, Billing, Publishing, Connections, Analytics, Technical problem, Feature request,
   **General question**, or **Security or private account matter** — *Only you and people you add can see this request* (workspace owners can't).
3. Add a **Subject** and **Message**.
4. Optional: **Attach screenshot or file** — PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, PDF or plain text, up to 10 MB each, five per message.
   Files are checked before they can be sent (*Files are still being checked*).
5. **Include technical details** is on by default: app version, browser family, the page you came from (path only) and
   recent error references. Nothing else — no cookies, tokens, storage or drafts. Untick to leave it out.
6. Select **Send**. **Result:** *Sent · RLY-482133* — keep the reference.

Passwords, API keys, tokens or card numbers pasted into a message are redacted before anyone sees them. Don't send them anyway.

![Contact support form with Category, Subject, Message, an attachment button for images, PDF or text up to 10 MB, and the Include technical details option](/help/img/support/contact-support.png)

## When Relay replies

You get an in-app notification and, if your preferences allow (**Relay support replies**), an email with the reference
and a link — never the message text. Reply in Relay; replying to the email isn't supported.
