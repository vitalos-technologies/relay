> **Note:** The website chat is turned on by Relay. When it's off you won't see the button; signed-in customers can always
> use **Help → Contact support** in the app.

## Send a message

1. On Relay's home page, select **Send us a message** (or **Chat with Relay support** when the team is staffing it live).
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email**, a **Topic** and your **Message**; attach files if useful (image, PDF or text). Signed-in
   customers write as themselves (*Signed in as …*) and choose the **Workspace**.
3. Select **Send message**. *We'll reply here and email you when we do.*

Replies appear in the chat on this browser. Relay emails your address a link when it replies — never the message text.

## Continue on another device

Select **Continue from another device** (or *Wrote to us from another device? Continue here*), enter the email you used,
and enter the 6-digit code or open the one-time link from the email (valid 15 minutes). A code is only sent if that
address has conversations.

## Privacy

- Typing an email doesn't prove who you are; it's only where replies go. Your website conversations are never merged into
  a Relay account or workspace.
- The chat doesn't show online status, typing or read indicators.
