## Profile

**Settings → Personal → Profile** ([/settings/personal/profile](/settings/personal/profile)) shows your name and email.
*Name and email come from your sign-in account* — change them at your identity provider; Relay picks them up the next
time you sign in.

## Appearance

**Settings → Personal → Appearance**, or the account menu → **Appearance**: **System** (follows your device), **Dark** or
**Light**. The choice is remembered on this browser.

## Accessibility

- **Keyboard:** every action works with the keyboard. **Ctrl K / ⌘ K** opens search; the crop tool, media ordering and
  the planner have keyboard alternatives to dragging (arrow buttons, the **⋯** menu, **Reschedule**).
- **Screen readers:** controls have names, statuses are written in text (never colour alone — for example conflicts in
  the planner), and live updates are announced politely.
- **Motion:** Relay follows your device's *reduce motion* setting.
- **Zoom and small screens:** pages are tested at 200% zoom and at phone widths from 320 px without sideways scrolling.
- **Time:** times always show their time zone, and your own time when it differs.
