## Steps

1. Open the link from your invitation email (or the link a colleague sent you). The page shows **Join <workspace>**, the
   role you were given, a masked version of the invited email address and when the invitation expires.
2. Select **Accept**.
3. Sign in with **the invited email address**. If you don't have a Relay sign-in yet, the sign-in page lets you create
   one where your organisation allows it; your email address must be verified.
4. **Result:** you land in the workspace. If you already belong to other workspaces, you can switch between them from the
   workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Sign in with the invited email / Use the invited email address | You signed in with a different address. | Sign out, then accept again with the invited address. |
| Expired. Ask for a new one. | The link is older than the workspace allows (72 hours by default). | Ask the person who invited you to resend it. |
| Revoked. | The invitation was withdrawn. | Ask the workspace admin. |
| Already accepted. | You already joined. | Just sign in. |
| No longer valid — ask for a new one | The invitation can no longer be used (for example the sender lost the right to grant your role). | Ask for a new invitation. |
| You verified as a different account | The identity provider signed you in as someone else. | Sign out of the identity provider and try again. |

## Good to know

- Some roles (Owner, Admin, Publisher and Brand manager by default) need **two-step verification** before you can do
  sensitive things such as scheduling. You can still read everything until you set it up; see
  [Set up two-step verification](/help/account-security/set-up-two-step-verification).
- A guest invitation gives access only to reviews shared with you, and only until the date shown.
