## Internal or shared

Every discussion has a visibility you choose before posting:

- **Internal**: your team only. Needs `comments.internal` (all staff roles). Guests never see these.
- **Shared**: also the client guests you shared the review with. Needs `comments.shared` (Owner, Admin, Brand manager,
  Editor, Approver; guests when allowed). Publishers, analysts and viewers can read shared discussions but can't start or
  reply to them.

A reply always has the visibility of its discussion. **Comments never approve a post**, whatever they say.

## Start a discussion

1. Open the post and its **Collaborate** panel → **Discussion** (on phones and tablets, tap **Collaborate** in the post header).
2. Choose **Internal** or **Shared** under *Who can see this*.
3. Optional: select text in the editor and choose **Comment on selection** to attach the quote.
4. Type your comment. Type **@** and a name to mention someone; only people who can see this visibility are offered.
5. Select **Comment**.

**Result:** mentioned people get one notification. Editing the comment later only notifies people you newly mention.

## Work with discussions

- **Reply**, **Resolve** or **Reopen**. Filter **Open** / **Resolved**.
- **Edit** (the old text stays under **Edit history**) and **Delete** (*Its text and edit history are removed. A
  placeholder stays in the discussion.*). You can edit or delete your own comments; brand managers can delete staff comments.
- **Outdated**: the quoted text has since been edited away. **From v3**: started on an earlier version, quote still present.
- **Share with client…** makes an internal discussion visible to clients. Relay shows exactly how many comments will
  become visible before you confirm. **Make internal…** hides it again; notifications clients already received stay in
  their inbox.

## Privacy

- Notification emails never contain comment or post text, only what happened and a link.
- Comments are never sent to the AI assistant or to MCP clients as instructions.

## Limitations

- Mentions don't grant access; you can only mention people who can already see the discussion.
- Clients can reply on shared discussions only while the workspace allows client comments and the review is open.
