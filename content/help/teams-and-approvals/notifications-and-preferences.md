## Read notifications

- The **bell** in the header shows your latest items and the unread count. Select an item to go straight to the
  discussion, task, review or post. Opening a link only navigates; it never approves or publishes anything.
- **All notifications** ([/notifications](/notifications)) shows the full list with a type filter and **Unread only**.
- Mark items **read / unread** one by one, or **Mark all read**.

If you lose access to the thing a notification is about, the notification disappears from your list.

## Choose how you're notified

1. Open **Settings → Personal → Notifications** ([/settings/personal/notifications](/settings/personal/notifications)).
2. Under **Channels per notification type**, tick **In app**, **Email** and/or **Digest** for each type:

| Type | When |
|---|---|
| Mentions | Someone mentions you. |
| Replies and client comments | A reply in a discussion you're in; a client comments on your review. |
| Review requests | You're eligible for a review stage, or someone asks you specifically. |
| Review decisions | Your post is approved, changes are requested, or a review is reset. |
| Tasks and assignments | A task or review is assigned to you; a task you follow changes. |
| Deadline reminders | A task is due soon or overdue; a review passes its review-by date. |
| Publishing problems | A delivery failed or needs attention. |
| Account health | A connected account needs reconnecting or is expiring. |
| Budgets | A usage budget is near or at its cap. |
| Relay support replies | Relay support answered one of your requests. |

3. **Quiet hours:** turn on and set **From** / **Until**. *Emails wait until morning. Security alerts still arrive.*
   In-app notifications are never delayed.
4. **Digest at:** the time of the daily digest (*One email a day with titles only*), and your **Time zone** (quiet hours
   and the digest use it). Until you save one, Relay uses UTC; the list may then show the first zone alphabetically
   (*Africa/Abidjan*), so pick your own zone before saving.
5. Select **Save**.

Until you choose, the workspace defaults apply (set by admins in **Collaboration settings**).

## What emails contain

Only what happened and a link back to Relay — never comment text, post text or task details. Replying to an email
isn't supported; reply in Relay.

## Limitations

- Emails need email delivery to be set up for your Relay installation. Without it you still get every in-app
  notification; admins see undelivered emails in **Collaboration → Notification delivery**.
- There are no browser push or mobile push notifications.
