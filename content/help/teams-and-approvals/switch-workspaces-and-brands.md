## Workspaces, brands and teams

- A **workspace** is your organisation's space in Relay: its members, plan and billing, settings and audit log. You can
  belong to several (for example your agency and a client's own workspace), each with its own roles.
- A **brand** lives inside a workspace and groups connected social accounts, posts, media, approval rules and analytics
  for one client or product. Roles can cover all brands or one brand.
- A **team** is a group of members in one workspace that shares access (see
  [Use teams](/help/teams-and-approvals/teams-and-delegated-administration)).

## Switch workspace

1. Select the workspace name at the top of the sidebar (on phones: **More**, then the workspace name).
2. Pick another workspace. Each entry shows your role there; a flask icon marks a **Demo** workspace with simulated destinations.
3. **Result:** the app reloads in that workspace. What you can see follows your roles there.

**New workspace…** appears at the bottom only if your Relay installation lets you create workspaces; otherwise ask a
workspace owner to invite you.

## Filter to one brand

1. Use the **Brand filter** in the header (**All brands** by default).
2. Pick a brand. Planner, library, analytics and brand-scoped settings follow the filter.

You only see brands where you hold a role. Guests never see brands; they only see reviews shared with them.

## Search and jump

Press **Ctrl K** (Windows, Linux) or **⌘ K** (Mac), or select **Search**. Type to find posts, campaigns, media, brands and
settings; use the arrow keys and **Enter**. You can also **Create draft** in a brand or **Open planner** from here. Results
only include things you can open. *Partial results* means the list was cut short — type more.

## Banners you may see

| Banner | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Demo · simulated destinations | This workspace publishes to simulated accounts only. Nothing reaches a real network. |
| Publishing paused | Someone paused publishing for the workspace (see [Pause publishing](/help/calendar-and-scheduling/pause-publishing-in-an-emergency)). |
| Relay support has read-only access | You approved a support-access request; it ends automatically. |
| Paid actions paused | A billing problem restricts paid actions (see [Billing restrictions](/help/billing-and-plans/when-paid-actions-are-paused)). |
| Offline | Your browser lost its connection; drafts are kept locally until you're back. |
