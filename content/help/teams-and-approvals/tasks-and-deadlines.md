## Who can do what

- **See tasks:** staff with `content.read` on the task's brand. Guests never see tasks.
- **Create tasks:** people who edit, submit, review or manage the brand.
- **Assign to someone else:** the same people, unless an admin limited it to *Brand managers and admins only*.
- **Be assigned:** active staff who can read the brand.
- **Change a task:** its creator, its assignee or a brand manager.

## Create a task

1. On a post: **Collaborate → Tasks → New task**. Anywhere else: **Tasks** ([/tasks](/tasks)) → **New task**, then pick
   the **Brand**.
2. Enter the **Task** title (for example *Check the caption*) and optional **Details**.
3. Choose an **Assignee** (search people, **Me**, or **Unassigned**).
4. Optional: a **Deadline** with its **Time zone**, and a **Priority**.
5. Select **Create task**.

**Result:** the assignee is notified. They get one reminder before the deadline (default 24 hours) and at most a few
reminders once it's overdue (default 3, at least a day apart).

## Work on a task

Open a task to **Start**, **Mark done**, mark **Blocked**, **Reopen** or **Cancel task**; reassign it; move or **Clear**
the deadline; change **Priority**; follow **Open post** / **Open review**; and read its **History**.

If two people change a task at once, the second sees "Someone else changed this task — Showing the latest." and nothing
is overwritten.

## Track workload

The **Tasks** page shows counters (**Assigned to me**, **Overdue**, **Due this week**, **Created by me**), filters
**Show** (Assigned to me, Created by me, Team), **Status** (Open, Done) and **Due** (Any time, Overdue, Next 7 days, No
deadline, **Needs a new assignee**), and a **Team workload** panel with open tasks per person.

## Consequences to know

- A task deadline is **editorial only**. It never moves when a post is rescheduled or published, and never changes a schedule.
- **Completing a task never approves or publishes the post.**
- When an assignee loses access, the task is flagged **Needs a new assignee** (and, depending on the workspace setting,
  unassigned). Admins can bulk-move such tasks in **Collaboration → Reassign work**.
