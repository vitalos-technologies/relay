**14-day free trial; no card needed**.

## Monthly allowances (defaults)

Allowances are per workspace per billing month: a base amount plus an amount per paid seat. They are never "unlimited",
and **no overage is ever charged**: at a limit, the action waits or is refused.

| Allowance | Standard | Professional | During the trial (whole trial) |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI tokens | 100,000 + 50,000 per seat | 500,000 + 200,000 per seat | 100,000 |
| Media storage | 10 GB + 5 GB per seat | 50 GB + 10 GB per seat | 2 GB |
| Exports | 20 | 200 | 5 |
| Connected social accounts | 5 + 3 per seat | 10 + 5 per seat | 5 |
| Active listening searches | — | 5 + 2 per seat | 2 (Professional trial) |
| Business-app sync jobs | — | 5,000 + 1,000 per seat | 200 (Professional trial) |
| MCP calls | — | 10,000 + 2,000 per seat | 500 (Professional trial) |
| Paid X API reads and writes | Workspace safety caps only | Workspace safety caps only | Not included |

Your workspace's current numbers are in **Settings → Workspace → Plan and billing** and **Settings → Operations → Usage
and limits**. Relay may adjust allowances for a workspace; this is shown as *Adjusted by Relay*.

## Who is a billable user

Every **active, accepted member**, including the owner. **Not billed:** pending invitations, Relay support staff present
through a support-access grant, service accounts, and up to **5** guests who only review shared posts (more than five
guests count as seats). A paid subscription has at least one seat.

## Older workspaces

Workspaces created before plans existed are on **Legacy** (*Your existing access, unchanged*): Professional features,
not limited by plan allowances. Legacy can't be bought.
