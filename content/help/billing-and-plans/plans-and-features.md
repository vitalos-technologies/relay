Relay has three plans. **Free** is £0 for one person. **Standard** and **Professional** are billed **monthly per billable
user** in GBP and each starts with a **14-day free trial; no card needed**. Any tax is shown at checkout.

| Publishing, scheduling, Planner, composer, media library | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

**On every plan:** your existing content is never locked or deleted because of a plan change, and you can always export
your workspace data.

## Limits and monthly allowances (defaults)

Monthly allowances reset each billing month: a base amount plus an amount per paid seat. They are never "unlimited", and
**no overage is ever charged**: at a limit, the action waits or is refused.

| Limit or allowance | Free | Standard | Professional | During the trial (whole trial) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Users | 1 | Billed per user | Billed per user | No seat limit |
| Brands | 1 | No plan limit | No plan limit | No plan limit |
| Connected social accounts | 2 | 5 + 3 per seat | 10 + 5 per seat | 5 |
| Scheduled posts a month | 10 | No plan limit | No plan limit | No plan limit |
| Media storage | 500 MB | 10 GB + 5 GB per seat | 50 GB + 10 GB per seat | 2 GB |
| AI tokens a month | 20,000 | 100,000 + 50,000 per seat | 500,000 + 200,000 per seat | 100,000 |
| Report exports a month | — | 20 | 200 | 5 |
| Active listening searches | — | — | 5 + 2 per seat | 2 (Professional trial) |
| Business-app sync jobs | — | — | 5,000 + 1,000 per seat | 200 (Professional trial) |
| MCP calls | — | — | 10,000 + 2,000 per seat | 500 (Professional trial) |
| Paid X API reads and writes | Not included | Workspace safety caps only | Workspace safety caps only | Not included |

"No plan limit" still means workspace safety caps apply. Your workspace's current numbers are in **Settings → Workspace →
Plan and billing** and **Settings → Operations → Usage and limits**. Relay may adjust allowances for a workspace; this is
shown as *Adjusted by Relay*.

## Moving to Free

A trial that ends without a subscription, or a subscription whose cancellation takes effect, moves the workspace to
**Free**. Anything over Free's limits — extra members, brands, connected accounts, scheduled posts — is **paused, not
deleted**, until an owner chooses what stays active or upgrades. See
[When paid actions are paused](/help/billing-and-plans/when-paid-actions-are-paused).

## Who is a billable user

On Standard and Professional, every **active, accepted member**, including the owner. **Not billed:** pending
invitations, Relay support staff present through a support-access grant, service accounts, and up to **5** guests who
only review shared posts (more than five guests count as seats). A paid subscription has at least one seat. Free has one
user and no teammates.

## Older workspaces

Workspaces created before plans existed are on **Legacy** (*Your existing access, unchanged*): Professional features,
not limited by plan allowances. Legacy can't be bought.
