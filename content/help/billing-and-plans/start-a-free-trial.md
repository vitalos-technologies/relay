## Steps

1. On Relay's home page, choose a plan: **Start free** on Free, or **Start free trial** on Standard or Professional (or
   **Start a free trial** on the sign-in page). For a paid plan the page shows your estimate, for example
   *3 users × £5 = £15 / month on Standard, after the 14-day trial*. Free shows no trial and no total.
2. Select **Continue with Free** or **Start free trial on <Plan>**. Sign in, or create a sign-in at the identity provider
   (your email must be verified).
3. **Create your workspace** — choose the **Plan**, a **Workspace name** and a **Time zone**, then **Create workspace**
   (Free) or **Start free trial** (*Your 14-day free trial starts now. No card needed.*).
4. **Result:** you're the workspace **Owner**. On Free you land on the Overview. On a trial, **Plan and billing** shows
   *Your 14-day free trial has started*, the trial end date, billable users and the monthly total after the trial.

Then: connect accounts, invite your team and create your first post (see **Getting started**).

## During the trial

- Every feature of the chosen plan, within **trial allowances** (for example 100,000 AI tokens and 2 GB storage for the
  whole trial). Paid X API usage isn't included.
- You can **switch the trial plan**; the end date stays the same.
- Subscribe any time; the **first charge is on the day the trial ends**.

## When the trial ends without a subscription

The workspace moves to **Free**. Anything over Free's limits (extra members, brands, connected accounts, scheduled posts)
and features Free doesn't include are **paused, not deleted**, until an owner chooses what stays active or subscribes.
Your data, settings, billing and support stay available, and data export always works. Subscribing restores everything
and releases held work that is still on time. See
[When paid actions are paused](/help/billing-and-plans/when-paid-actions-are-paused).

## Limitations

- One trial per workspace. Joining someone else's workspace, refreshing, or starting checkout again never creates or
  extends a trial.
- Free has one user: to invite teammates, subscribe to Standard or Professional.
- If you already belong to a workspace, the trial page sends you to that workspace's billing (*You already have a
  workspace*) instead of starting another trial; ask an owner to invite you to new workspaces.
- Self-service sign-up must be enabled for your Relay installation.
