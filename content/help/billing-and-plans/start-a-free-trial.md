## Steps

1. On Relay's home page, choose a plan and select **Start free trial** (or **Start a free trial** on the sign-in page).
   The page shows your estimate, for example *3 users × £10 = £30 / month on Standard, after the 14-day trial*.
2. Select **Start free trial on <Plan>**. Sign in, or create a sign-in at the identity provider (your email must be verified).
3. **Create your workspace** — *Your 14-day free trial starts now. No card needed.* Choose the **Plan to try**, a
   **Workspace name** and a **Time zone**, then **Start free trial**.
4. **Result:** you're the workspace **Owner**, and **Plan and billing** shows *Your 14-day free trial has started*, the
   trial end date, billable users and the monthly total after the trial.

Then: connect accounts, invite your team and create your first post (see **Getting started**).

![Plan and billing during a trial in test mode: Standard plan, Free trial with 14 days left, 1 seat, £10 monthly total excluding tax, payment method not needed for the trial, and Standard and Professional plan cards with a seat stepper](/help/img/billing-and-plans/plan-and-billing.png)

## During the trial

- Every feature of the chosen plan, within **trial allowances** (for example 100,000 AI tokens and 2 GB storage for the
  whole trial). Paid X API usage isn't included.
- You can **switch the trial plan**; the end date stays the same.
- Subscribe any time; the **first charge is on the day the trial ends**.

## When the trial ends without a subscription

The workspace becomes **restricted**: paid actions pause and scheduled posts are **held, not published or deleted**.
Your data, settings, billing, support and exports stay available. Subscribing restores everything and releases held work
that is still on time. See [When paid actions are paused](/help/billing-and-plans/when-paid-actions-are-paused).

## Limitations

- One trial per workspace. Joining someone else's workspace, refreshing, or starting checkout again never creates or
  extends a trial.
- If you already belong to a workspace, the trial page sends you to that workspace's billing (*You already have a
  workspace*) instead of starting another trial; ask an owner to invite you to new workspaces.
- Self-service sign-up must be enabled for your Relay installation.
