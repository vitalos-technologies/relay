| You see | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Expired. Ask for a new one. | Older than the workspace allows (72 hours by default). | Ask the admin to **Resend** — that creates a new link. |
| Revoked. / No longer valid — ask for a new one | Withdrawn, or replaced by a resend (old links stop working), or the sender lost the right to invite with that role. | Ask for a new invitation. |
| Sign in with the invited email. | You signed in with another address. | Sign out of the identity provider and accept with the invited address. |
| Already accepted. | You've joined already. | Sign in normally and switch to that workspace. |
| Couldn't accept — membership suspended | You were suspended in that workspace. | Ask an admin to reactivate you; invitations can't. |

**Admins:** if email isn't set up for your Relay installation, invitations aren't emailed. Copy the **Invitation link
(shown once)** from the dialog and send it yourself. See [Resend, revoke or replace an invitation](/help/teams-and-approvals/manage-invitations).
