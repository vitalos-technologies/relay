Relay never publishes late: if a post can't start within its **deadline** (scheduled time + the deadline window, 15 minutes
by default), it **expires** instead.

| Status | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Overdue** | The publisher is behind, off for maintenance, or not running (see the Overview banner). | Usually nothing — it goes out shortly if still within its deadline. Admins: **Operations**. |
| **Paused** | Workspace, brand or account paused. | Resume. Posts still before their deadline then send. |
| **Awaiting approval** | Not approved yet. | Ask a reviewer; approval after the deadline is refused. |
| **Blocked** — authority hold | The person who scheduled or approved it lost access. | **Team → Held posts → Adopt**, or resubmit. |
| **Blocked** — billing or plan | Paid actions paused, or the account is over the plan limit. | Fix billing / choose active accounts. |
| **Blocked** — account | Account needs reconnecting or lacks permissions. | Reconnect in Connections, then retry. |
| **Expired** (*Missed deadline*) | Couldn't start in time for any of the above reasons. | **Retry** (if still relevant) or **Reschedule**. |

To avoid surprises: keep accounts healthy (**Overview → Channel health**), resolve reviews early, and choose a longer
**Deadline** for posts that may still go out a little later.
