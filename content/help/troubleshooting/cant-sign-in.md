| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Forgot your password | Relay has no passwords. Enter your email and use the link or 6-digit code Relay sends you, or **Continue with Google**. |
| The sign-in email doesn't arrive | Wait a minute and check spam, then select **Send a new code**. Use the address you were invited with: if it has no Relay account, nothing is sent. Still nothing? Your administrator should check the sign-in server's email settings. |
| The link says it expired or was already used | Links work once, for 15 minutes, in the browser where you asked for them. Type the 6-digit code instead, or start again. |
| *Link expired. Start again.* | Start again from the sign-in page in one tab. |
| *No workspace yet. Ask an admin to invite you.* | Your sign-in works but you're not in a workspace. Ask for an invitation, or start a free trial. |
| You're in, but the workspace you expect is missing | You were removed, or the invitation used another email. Ask the workspace admin. |
| *Your access to this workspace is suspended. Contact the workspace owner.* | An admin suspended you. Only they can reactivate you. |
| Lost your phone or passkey | [Use a recovery code](/help/account-security/recovery-codes-and-lost-factors). |
| *You verified as a different account* | You're signed in at the identity provider as someone else. Sign out there and retry. |
| *Session ended* keeps appearing | Sessions end after 30 minutes idle. Check your device clock and that cookies are allowed for Relay. |
| *Identity provider error* | Try again in a few minutes; if it continues, contact your admin or Relay support. |

Your email must be **verified** at the identity provider before Relay can sign you in or match an invitation.
