1. Open **Planner → History** ([/history](/history)).
2. Filter by **Network** (All networks, or one), **Result** (All results, or one status) and a **From** / **To** date.
3. The table shows **Post**, **Destination**, **Result**, **Scheduled**, **Completed** and **Attempts**.
4. Select a row to see details, including **Published (UTC)** and the network's **Provider IDs**, and **Open post**.

Everyone with read access to a brand can see its history. **CSV** (top right) downloads the filtered list; it needs
`exports.create` (Owner, Admin, Exporter) and counts toward the export allowance.

*Updating…* means new results arrived while you were looking; the list refreshes.
