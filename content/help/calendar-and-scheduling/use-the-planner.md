Open **Planner** ([/planner](/planner)). Everyone with a role on a brand can view it; what you can change depends on your role.

## Views and navigation

- **View**: **Week**, **Month** (larger screens) or **List** (all screens; the only view on phones).
- **Previous**, **Next** and **Today** move through time. Times are shown in each post's own time zone.
- **Queue** and **History** in the header open the content queue (when turned on) and the delivery history.

## Filters

Pick a brand in the header first to filter by **Account**. Then use **Campaign**, **Team or owner** and **Status**
(Scheduled, Awaiting approval, Publishing, Published, Partial success, Needs attention, Failed, Expired, Cancelled).
If there are too many posts, the planner shows *Showing the first N posts — Narrow by account or status to see the rest.*

## Conflicts

Two posts to the **same destination within 15 minutes** of each other are marked as a conflict (with an icon and text,
not just colour). Select **N in conflict** to show only those; select it again to show all.

## Work with a post

- Select a post to open the **Details** drawer: owner, schedule time and zone, destinations with their status, and the
  base text. From there: **Duplicate**, **Reschedule**, **Open post**.
- **Drag** a post to another day to move it (people who can schedule on that brand). A message *Moved "…"* offers
  **Undo**. Approved posts that move need approval again.
- Select several posts to use [bulk actions](/help/publishing/bulk-actions), such as reschedule by an offset.

## Good to know

- **Simulated · delivered** marks posts sent to simulated (demo) accounts. Nothing reached a real network.
- The planner never changes a post's content; moving it changes only its time.
