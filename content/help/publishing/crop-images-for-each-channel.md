1. Open the post and select a destination in the content switcher.
2. Under **Crop** (*Only for this destination. The exact cropped image is what gets sent.*), choose the image.
3. Pick an **Aspect ratio**: **1:1**, **4:5**, **1.91:1**, **16:9**, **9:16** or **free**.
4. Drag the crop area, or use the keyboard: arrow keys move it, **Shift** moves faster, **Alt** + arrows resizes.
5. Select **Apply**. **Remove crop** goes back to the full image.

**Result:** the **Preview** for that destination shows the cropped image, and that exact crop is what the network receives.

## Good to know

- Crops are stored with the post version. Changing a crop on a submitted post resets its review.
- The library file isn't changed; other posts and destinations still use the full image unless cropped there too.
- Crops don't apply to video.
