1. Open the post → **⋯ More post actions → Version history**, or **Collaborate → History**.
2. **Versions** lists every saved version, newest first, with who saved it. **Current** marks the draft; **Submitted
   versions** (*Submitted for review or scheduled*) are the exact versions that were reviewed or sent. **Unsaved copy**
   marks a version Relay kept when your save clashed with someone else's.
3. Select **Compare** on a version. Relay shows differences in **Title**, **Text**, **Link**, **Media**, **Destinations**
   (**Added** / **Removed**) and **Schedule**.
4. To go back, select **Restore this** → **Restore as new version**.

**Result:** *Restored as a new version.* Nothing is deleted; the restored content becomes the newest version.

## Consequences

- *Earlier approvals don't carry over.* On a review brand, a restored version needs a new review.
- Restoring doesn't schedule or publish anything, and never changes what was already sent.
- Discussions started on a version are flagged on it (*Open discussions started on this version*).

**Load older versions** shows more. Viewing history needs read access; restoring needs edit access.
