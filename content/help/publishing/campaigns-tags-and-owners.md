## Campaigns

1. Open **Campaigns** ([/campaigns](/campaigns)) and choose the brand.
2. Select **New campaign**: **Name**, optional **Starts** and **Ends** (*End must be after start*), **Description**.
3. Each campaign's **⋯** menu offers **Edit**, **Archive** / **Restore** and, when campaign links are on, **Measurement**.
   **Show** switches between active and archived campaigns.

Put a post in a campaign from its **Collaborate** panel → **Tasks** → *Post owner and editorial deadline* (**Campaign**,
or **Add campaign** to create one on the spot), or for many posts with **Set campaign** in [bulk actions](/help/publishing/bulk-actions). Filter the planner by **Campaign**.

## Owner (assignee) and team

In the same place (**Collaborate → Tasks → Post owner and editorial deadline**) set **Assignee** (*Only people with access
to this brand*), an editorial **Due** date in the brand's time zone, and **Team** (*No team*). The due date is editorial
only; it never changes a schedule. The planner's **Team or owner**
filter and the Details drawer show them. Making someone the owner doesn't give them access; they must already be able to
read the brand.

## Tags

Add tags to posts (bulk **Add tags** / **Remove tags**, or via CSV import) and to media files (file details). Tags help
search, filters and the media picker.

## Limitations

- Campaigns belong to one brand.
- Archived campaigns stay attached to their posts; they just aren't offered for new ones.
