## Before you start

- **Required permission:** `content.create` / `content.edit` on the brand (Editor, Brand manager, Admin, Owner).
  Without it the **Create** page says *View-only access*.
- The brand needs at least one connected account to pick destinations (Owners and Admins connect them in
  **Connections**). You can still write a draft without destinations.

## Start a post

1. Select **Create** in the sidebar ([/compose](/compose)).
2. Under **New post**, choose the **Brand**. Banners tell you if the brand is **paused** or if **Approval required**.
3. Optional: choose a **Template** (or keep **Blank post**).
4. Select **Create draft**. Your post opens in the editor.

The **Drafts** list on the same page shows your brand's drafts with when they were last edited. You can also import
posts from a spreadsheet (**Import CSV**, see [Import posts from a CSV file](/help/publishing/import-posts-from-csv)) or
from Google Docs and Notion (**From Drive or Notion**).

## Write it

1. **Post title (internal)** — *Internal title — never published.* Helps you find it later.
2. **Destinations** — tick the connected accounts this post should go to. Use **Search destinations** when there are many.
3. **Base content** — the shared copy every destination uses unless you customise it:
   - **Text** (*Write your post…*). **Length per destination** shows how much of each network's limit you use.
   - **CTA**, **Hashtags** and **Footer** buttons (shown when the brand voice defines them) append the brand's defaults;
     an **Avoided terms** banner warns about words the brand avoids.
   - **Link** (optional) — a full `https://` address, *appended to the text where a network has no link field*.
   - **Media** — images, an album or one video (see [Add images, albums and video](/help/publishing/add-images-albums-and-video)).
4. Select a destination in the content switcher to customise its version (see
   [Shared copy and channel versions](/help/publishing/shared-copy-and-channel-versions)).
5. Check **Readiness** and **Preview** on the right (on phones: the **Preview** step).

On phones the editor has three steps: **Destinations**, **Write**, **Preview** (with the number of issues).

## Saving

- Relay saves automatically shortly after you stop typing. The status shows **Saving…**, *Saved 14:02. Version history*,
  **Unsaved** or **Saved locally**.
- **Save draft** (in **⋯**) saves immediately.
- Offline? *You're offline. Drafting only.* Your text is kept in this browser and saved when you're back
  (see [Recover unsaved changes](/help/publishing/recover-unsaved-changes)).

## Common errors

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| Pick a destination. | Tick at least one destination before scheduling or publishing. |
| Resolve the conflict first. | Someone else saved a newer version; see [Edit at the same time](/help/publishing/edit-at-the-same-time). |
| Your access to this post changed | Reload; your role changed while you were editing. |
| This brand is archived | Archived brands can't get new posts. |
